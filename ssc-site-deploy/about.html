About S.S.C. Installations

A specialty contracting firm built on one idea: handle the equipment work other contractors won't touch, and do it without cutting corners.

We take a no-nonsense approach — installations done right, on time, and built to last.

S.S.C. Installations will provide our customers with high quality service and a straightforward approach to business. We consider ourselves partners with our customers, employees, and suppliers — and that philosophy, mixed with over a century of combined team experience, is what keeps projects on schedule and on budget.

Founded in 1995, S.S.C. Installations is a contracting firm that specializes in everything from safe moving to full ATM and vault installation. Bank equipment installation and associated general contracting have long been our foundation — but our team's background in electrical work, steel fabrication, and heavy rigging means we take on projects for retailers, pharmacies, and homeowners just as readily as we do for financial institutions. We manage electrical, structural, and logistical work under one roof, so you get a single point of contact instead of coordinating five different contractors.

Our reputation is built on quality installations, on-time project completion, competitive pricing, and total customer satisfaction. S.S.C. Installations, Inc. holds a Commercial Electrical License.
